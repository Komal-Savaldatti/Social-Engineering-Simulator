SOCIAL ENGINEERING SIMULATOR

Cyber Security Awareness & Phishing Simulation Project

About Project

The Social Engineering Simulator is developed to create awareness about phishing attacks, fake login pages, credential theft and social engineering techniques. This project helps users understand how attackers manipulate people to steal confidential information.

The simulator provides educational phishing demonstrations to train users in identifying suspicious links, fake websites and malicious login pages. It also maintains logs of user simulation activities and improves cyber security awareness among users.
